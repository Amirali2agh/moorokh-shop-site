# Design
The demo uses a soft rose-and-white palette with a deep berry announcement bar, white RTL header, blush hero, photo-based category shortcuts, selected-products shelf, and responsive product grid. Rounded controls and warm pink surfaces give it a softer beauty-store feel while preserving the catalog flow. Cards show product photography, a key specification, price enquiry and an add-to-cart control. Product details expose specs with a quantity-aware cart control. Browse → add from card/details → responsive cart with quantity controls, item removal, and a summary; cart and favorites persist in local storage. No order/payment is submitted. CSS tokens are in `public/shop.css`.
