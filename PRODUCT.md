# Binux Shop Demo
Persian RTL catalog demo showcasing Sweet Girl hair-care products. The 14 product listings, brochure-extracted product photos, and brochure-derived marketing specifications are provided for a familiar browse-and-select journey. Product claims are not independently verified; no prices were included in the source brochure and they display as “استعلام قیمت”. No backend, payment gateway, personal-data collection, or real order submission is present. Cart/favorites are local-browser demo features. Serve only `public/`; the original brochure is not part of the repository.
